Hi team, I wanted to share some notes from yesterday's planning meeting, since a few of you could not make it. The big decision is that we are moving the launch from March tenth to March twenty fourth. The main reason is the payment integration, which still fails on about three percent of transactions, mostly for customers in Europe using older cards. Priya is going to own that fix, and she thinks it will take about a week and a half. In the meantime, the design team will finish the new dashboard, and we will run a beta with roughly two hundred users starting next Monday. On the infrastructure side, we are upgrading the Kubernetes nodes to the new instance type, which should cut our monthly bill by around eighteen percent. We also agreed to turn on Tailscale for all internal tools, so nobody needs the old VPN anymore. A couple of open questions remain. Do we want to keep the free tier, or switch to a fourteen day trial? And who is writing the announcement blog post? Please reply in the thread by Friday so we can lock the plan. Thanks everyone, and let me know if I missed anything important.
