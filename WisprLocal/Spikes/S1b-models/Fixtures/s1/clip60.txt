Here is the plan for the rest of the quarter. First, we are moving every internal service onto Kubernetes, with Helm charts stored in the platform repository and Argo CD handling deployments. Second, remote access will go through Tailscale instead of the old VPN, which means each laptop needs the client installed and approved by an administrator. Third, the product team is shipping a native Mac dictation app inspired by Wispr Flow. It runs Parakeet speech recognition locally on the Neural Engine, so audio never leaves the device, and it pastes the cleaned up text into whatever application has focus. Sarah Chen owns the audio pipeline, Diego Alvarez owns the settings window, and I will handle accessibility permissions and the global hotkey. We should have a beta ready for internal testing by the fifteenth of November, and a public release shortly after Thanksgiving if the crash rate stays below one percent.
